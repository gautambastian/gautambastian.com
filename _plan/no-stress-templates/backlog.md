# No Stress Templates — Product Backlog

## Core products (primary revenue)

1. **Zero-Based Budget Builder**  
   Google Sheets. Guided template where users rebuild their budget from zero, with auto-formatting and variance checks.

2. **Budget Forensics Toolkit**  
   Sheets + guide. For new EDs, board members, or consultants who need 2-hour diagnostic of organizational stress from raw financials.

3. **Grant Budget Source-Map**  
   Sheets + PDF. Maps every budget line from proposal narrative to funder line-item format; auto-checks for omissions.

4. **Project Budget Playbook**  
   Sheets. Multi-year project budget with phasing, indirect cost allocation, and burn-rate dashboard.

5. **Unstructured Data → Form 990 Reconciler**  
   Sheets + workflow guide. Paste CSV/PDF text, guided formulas pull out expenses/revenue into reconciled totals.

6. **Monthly Close Kit**  
   Sheets + 30-min onboarding doc. Close checklist, journal entry pad, variance analysis.

7. **Nonprofit KPI Dashboard**  
   Sheets. Program, fundraising, and admin KPI tracker for EDs/board.

8. **Capital Campaign Budget Canvas**  
   Sheets. Campaign-specific timeline + cash-flow + pledge tracker.

9. **Grant Writer’s Comp Pack**  
   PDF + Sheets. Budget narrative templates, logic model sheets, budget justification paragraph builder.

10. **School Finance Roadmap**  
    Sheets. Designed for charter schools: per-pupil budget allocator, grant-funded program tracker.
