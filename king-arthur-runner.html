Quests of the
Round Table

Guide Sir Lancelot through the medieval forest. Leap stump and knight alike, ride the ramps over the giants, and claim the Gold Lion Shield at fifty points.

Goal: 50 pointsLives: 3No timerNo audio, by design

Levels

Each level is a stretch of the endless run. A level unlocks once your best score reaches its start. Select any unlocked level to begin the quest from there.

Shields

Earn a new heraldic shield every ten points. The final reward is the Gold Lion Shield, borne by the champions of the Round Table.

Suits

All four tournament suits are granted at the start of the quest. Choose the armor Sir Lancelot wears into the forest.

How to Play

Jump

Press Space, ↑ or W, click, or tap the screen to jump.

Score

One point for every obstacle cleared. Reach 50 to win the quest.

Lives

Three lives per run. A collision costs one life and briefly stuns Lancelot.

Ramps

Run into a wooden ramp to launch high over the giant knights that no jump can clear.

Shields

Every ten points upgrades your heraldic shield, from the plain red shield to the Gold Lion.

Pause

Press P or the Pause button. Esc returns to the main menu.
