# Project Charter: No Stress Templates

**Owner:** Gautam Bastian  
**Operator:** Hermes Agent  
**Start:** 2026-06-23  
**Horizon:** 12 months

---

## Purpose

This document defines the scope of the **No Stress Templates** business lane. It exists to prevent drift. Everything not explicitly in-scope here belongs in the backlog or a separate project.

---

## Vision

A faceless digital product business selling finance and admin templates to nonprofits and small businesses. Products leverage unstructured financial data entry and presentation. Revenue path: template sales → productized consulting retainers.

---

## Objectives

- $2,000/month recurring by month 12.
- 4 core products shipped by month 8.
- Consulting pipeline with ≥5 inbound/month by month 6.
- Build once, sell forever. Minimal ongoing ops.

---

## Scope Statement

### In Scope

- Google Sheets / Excel templates, with optional PDF exports.
- Guided workbook format: templates + walkthrough instructions.
- AI-enhanced workflows: templates ship with embedded prompts and tool guidance.
- Pinterest, Reels/TikTok, Etsy, and later owned checkout (LemonSqueezy).
- Product-to-consulting upsell funnel.
- Future expansion to small business project budget lane (by month 4).
- Branded under **gautambastian.com** (with shop brand **No Stress Templates**).
- Sub-lanes: **No Stress Nonprofit**, **No Stress Schools**, **No Stress Small Business**.

### Out of Scope

- Face-based personal brand or video appearances.
- Custom client work, bespoke builds, or consulting engagements sold directly.
- Enterprise sales, RFPs, or decision-process sales.
- Notion-native templates at launch (revisit after month 6 based on buyer data).
- Micro-courses and PD workshop kits at launch (backlog tier 2).

### Decision Log

| Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Google Sheets/Excel as primary format | Maximum accessibility; buyers already use them. |
| No Notion at launch | Uncertain adoption in target nonprofit segment. |
| No face/faceless only | Comfort + defensibility; screen-capture + voiceover suffices. |
| AI embedded, not AI-only | Augments the workbook; doesn’t replace domain expertise. |
| Buy-and-use model | No RFPs, no custom sales process. Discovery-led purchases only. |
