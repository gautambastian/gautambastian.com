## 2. Platform Strategy

1. **Month 1:** Etsy listings + Pinterest static pins + 2 faceless Reels/week (screen-recorded previews + voiceover).
2. **Month 3:** Add email capture on product thank-you pages → migrate high-intent buyers to LemonSqueezy.
3. **Month 5:** YouTube tutorials (faceless screen capture) for each product.
4. **Month 8:** Outreach to nonprofit newsletters / grant writing communities for affiliate offers.

- **Monday:** business growth review — sales, content pipeline, consulting inbound.
- **Tuesday/Wednesday:** build day — specs, software, QA.

## 5. Consulting Funnel

“Stuck diagnosing your org’s financial stress? Book a 30-min ‘Budget Forensics Triage’ ($199).”
Convert to monthly close retainer ($499–999/month).

## 6. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Etsy fee/listing drag | Medium | Move to owned checkout by month 3. |
| Spreadsheet piracy | Medium | License in file, email-linked updates. |
| Market niche ceiling | Low-medium | Expand to “small business project budget” by month 4. |
| Competing free templates | High | Differentiate on walkthrough + unstructured entry + AI prompts. |
